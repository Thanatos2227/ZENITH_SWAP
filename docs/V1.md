# ZENITH V1 AMM Specification

## 1. Overview

Zenith V1 is an exact constant-product automated market maker ($x \cdot y = k$).

## 2. Core Contracts

- `ZenithV1Factory.sol`: Deploys and indexes deterministic pair contracts.
- `ZenithV1Pair.sol`: Holds pool reserves, calculates LP mint/burn, and executes token swaps.
- `ZenithV1Router.sol`: Helper for user token transfers, slippage checks, deadline guards, and multi-hop paths.

## 3. Mathematical Model

### Invariant:
$$(R_0 + \Delta a_{in} \cdot 0.997) \cdot (R_1 - \Delta a_{out}) \ge R_0 \cdot R_1$$

### Swap Formula:
$$\text{amountOut} = \frac{\text{amountIn} \cdot 997 \cdot \text{reserveOut}}{\text{reserveIn} \cdot 1000 + \text{amountIn} \cdot 997}$$

### Liquidity Minting:
$$\text{LP}_{\text{initial}} = \sqrt{\text{amount0} \cdot \text{amount1}} - 1000$$
$$\text{LP}_{\text{subsequent}} = \min\left(\frac{\text{amount0} \cdot \text{totalSupply}}{\text{reserve0}}, \frac{\text{amount1} \cdot \text{totalSupply}}{\text{reserve1}}\right)$$
